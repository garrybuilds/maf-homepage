The Method

Every business has one constraint. Not five priorities, not a strategic plan with twelve initiatives — one thing that, if fixed, unlocks everything else.

The phone isn't ringing enough. Or it's ringing but calls aren't converting. Or you're selling but can't deliver on time. Or you're delivering but cash flow chokes the next hire. The constraint sits somewhere in that chain — and it's findable.

The 6-vector scorecard maps your business across demand, conversion, delivery, cash flow, retention, and support. Each vector gets a score. The lowest score is your binding constraint — the one that holds all the others back. Fix it first. Everything else gets easier.

This isn't a dashboard for you to monitor. It's a diagnosis. We run it, we read it, we tell you what it means in plain language, and we deploy against what it finds. No software to learn. No platform to adopt. The diagnostic is free. The work is done for you.
