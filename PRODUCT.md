## Users

Owners and leaders of service-based businesses, ranging from field-service operators to large commercial general contractors, engineering firms, and other professional or operational service companies. Their businesses are built around serving customers and delivering real work; they are not trying to become technology companies. They want to harness AI and advanced systems without losing focus on their core business. They trust people who show up, do the work, and don't make promises they can't keep. They respond to specificity, directness, and evidence of understanding their operation — not to gradient backgrounds or "scale without limits."

Their situation: the phone isn't ringing like it used to. Revenue plateaued. They're working harder than ever but the business isn't growing. They suspect something is blocking them but can't name it. They've tried hiring more people, buying software, working longer hours — none of it moved the needle.

Their job: understand what is constraining performance and get an accountable, technically capable team to fix it while they remain focused on serving customers, delivering projects, growing the business, or preparing for an exit.

## Product Purpose

Malwa Asset Firm finds the one constraint limiting a service business's growth and deploys against it. The method is diagnostic-first: a free 6-vector scorecard (24 questions, 10 minutes) that surfaces the single binding constraint across demand, conversion, delivery, cash flow, retention, and support. Then the firm deploys against that constraint — either through AI operations (MAF Deploy), growth consulting, or M&A advisory.

Success means: the owner knows their constraint within 10 minutes, and within 60 days of engagement, measurable results against it — or a refund.

## Positioning

Not a software tool. Not a platform. Not a "solution." A forward-deployed team that does the work for you, on top of the systems you already run (ServiceTitan, QuickBooks, your phone system, your CRM). The diagnostic is free. The constraint is findable. The fix is deployable. The guarantee is real (refund every month we fail to deliver).

Three service lines, all starting from the same diagnostic:
1. **MAF Deploy** — AI agents that run your back office: scheduling, invoicing, lead follow-up, customer calls. Done for you, not sold to you.
2. **Consulting** — Growth and operations diagnosis: pricing, delivery capacity, cash flow, team structure. We build the plan.
3. **M&A Advisory** — For owners ready to transition: valuation, readiness, deal execution. We've sat on both sides of the table.
